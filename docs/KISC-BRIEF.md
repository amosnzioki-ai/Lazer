## 3. The main HR website and staff portals

I recommend six distinct interfaces, all running on the same secure system.

Group Oversight: Enko Education

Authorized regional/group HR staff can see consolidated workforce numbers, staffing budgets, vacancies, compliance completion and approved reports. Confidential employee information remains restricted according to policy.

KISC Head Office and Central HR

Full authorized KISC HR operations across the seven schools, including recruitment, contracts, leave, payroll coordination, employee records, workforce movement and central policies.

School Head and Local Administrator

School-specific workforce details, staff attendance, leave approvals, teacher cover requirements, probation reviews and assigned recruitment requests.

Staff Self-Service Portal

A simple space where each teacher and non-teaching employee can request leave, view payslips, check their schedule, update permitted details, view documents and complete assigned HR tasks.

Payroll and Finance

Authorized salary processing, deductions, allowances, payroll approvals, monthly reconciliation, financial reporting and statutory submissions.

Recruitment and Careers

An applicant-facing job portal and restricted recruitment tools for advertising vacancies, screening candidates, scheduling interviews and preparing staff appointments.

### Curriculum-specific staff management

Because KISC operates different curricula, the system should record each teacher's applicable qualifications, department and curriculum assignments.

British Curriculum HR Profile

Cambridge / Pearson subject specialization, relevant teacher qualifications, IGCSE/A-Level teaching assignments, curriculum-specific development, term arrangements, employment contracts, performance goals and professional training.

CBE Curriculum HR Profile

CBE teaching area, relevant professional qualifications, curriculum preparation, authorized teaching assignments, competency-focused professional development and teacher appraisal requirements.

Other or Transitional Curriculum Profiles

Configurable profiles for remaining 8-4-4-related roles, transitional arrangements and any future curriculum changes. HR can disable an obsolete curriculum category without losing historical employment records.

One teacher can have more than one curriculum assignment. A school's curricula should be configurable rather than permanently tied to an individual school in code.

The system should also distinguish academic terms, public holidays, contract working days and actual employee leave entitlements. A school holiday does not automatically mean every staff member is on annual leave.

## 6. Staff categories to support

Beyond teachers and school leaders, KISC should be able to manage employees such as HR and payroll staff, accountants, receptionists, administrative assistants, ICT technicians, librarians, laboratory technicians, drivers, transport coordinators, security staff, kitchen staff, cleaners, maintenance personnel, boarding operations staff and other support roles.

Each staff category can have its own employment conditions, approved working schedules, supervisor, training requirements and access permissions.

For example, a driver may require an appropriate driving licence and periodic renewal reminders. A laboratory technician may need safety training. A teacher may need professional registration and curriculum-specific development records.

Safeguarding-related staff training and required employee clearances may be recorded, but this system should not store learner safeguarding cases or student personal information.

## 8. Important school-specific workflows

A. Recruiting a British Curriculum teacher

Head of School requests vacancy → HR verifies approval → Recruitment posts job → Candidate screening and credential checks → Interview → Offer approval → Contract → Staff onboarding

B. Moving a staff member between schools

Transfer request → Current and receiving managers review → HR confirms position and effective date → Payroll cost allocation updates → New reporting line and access permissions → Historical record retained

C. Managing staff absences

Leave or absence reported → School receives notification → Authorized manager reviews staffing impact → Temporary staff coverage arranged if necessary → HR records decision → Attendance and payroll updated

D. Renewing teacher contracts

Advance expiry alert → School leadership review → Performance and staffing needs checked → HR prepares renewal → Required approval → Electronic acknowledgment or signature → Updated contract saved

## 9. Making it easy for KISC staff

For staff, the system should feel as straightforward as a mobile banking app.

The employee homepage should focus on five common needs: My Profile, My Leave, My Payslips, My Documents and My Requests.

I would also add a clear notification inbox, searchable help page and a prominent way to contact HR. Staff should not need to understand complex HR terms or navigate through many screens.

For head office, a global school filter should allow authorized HR users to switch between all seven school sections without repeated logins.

For school leaders, dashboards should automatically open to their own school and prioritize the matters requiring their attention.

For employees with limited internet connectivity, the design should use lightweight pages, allow documents to be downloaded, and avoid unnecessarily large files.

## 10. Security, ownership and privacy

Because KISC belongs to a wider school network, its design needs a particularly clear distinction between local employee data and parent-group oversight.

I would implement:

- Separate school, KISC-wide and Enko group access scopes.
- Mandatory MFA for administrators and payroll approvers.
- Restricted salary, bank, identity and sensitive staff records.
- Independent authorization for sensitive payroll and personnel changes.
- Full records of administrative changes and authorized downloads.
- Controlled group-level data access, not unrestricted visibility simply because the group owns the schools.
- Encrypted records, automated backups and tested restoration.
- Staff privacy notices, retention rules and procedures for correction or access requests.
- Special review of international data sharing between KISC and any group systems outside Kenya.

Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner sets out employees' data subject rights and publishes guidance on cross-border data transfers and data protection governance. These should inform the final design.&#x20;

# KISC PeopleHub

### One Team. Seven Schools. One Connected HR Experience.

A proposed digital Human Resource Management System for Kitengela International Schools, designed to simplify staff management, connect school-level HR operations with the head office, and support authorized Enko Education oversight.

Purpose: To help HR spend less time chasing forms, maintaining duplicate records and manually tracking approvals, and more time supporting employees across the KISC network.

Scope: Staff and HR management only.

Brand identity: KISC gold, eagle maroon and green.

Ownership: A proposed independent concept for KISC HR consideration.

### Recommended first version

I would prioritize the employee directory, seven-school structure, head office dashboard, admin roles, leave applications, staff attendance, contracts, professional qualifications, employee self-service, basic payroll coordination, reporting and complete audit logs.

Then I would expand it to include recruitment, appraisals, staff development, advanced payroll, Enko reporting and more sophisticated HR analytics.

This would give KISC a useful, coherent working system without making the initial version too complicated.
